# Formal Sponsorship / Funding Letter Template

* **Document Type:** Formal sponsorship letter  
* **Target Demographic:** Club Secretary / President (Sender), ECA Director (Receiver)  
* **Problem:** Delayed and/or inadequate funding due to miscommunication  
* **Goal:** Streamlining the communication process for funding  
                      
Below is a sample letter to send to the ECA director when the club requires sponshorship for an outside event. 

**What to include/replace:**
- Fill out the proper names, events, and dates in place of the placeholder angle brackets (`<EXAMPLE>`).
- Highlight the advantages of attending the event for both the club and the school. Note that the actual advantages may be vastly different from the ones written here.
- Ensure full transparency for the handling of the funds.
- Reassure that the mentors at hand (if any) are experienced and trustworthy.

## Template

---

> **Official Letterhead**

---

Date: `<DATE>`

Respected Sir/Madam,

We hope this letter finds you in good health. The `<CLUB_NAME>` is writing this letter in hopes of receiving **[SELECT ONE:** `<FUNDING_AMOUNT>` (for revenue funding) **OR** `<EQUIPMENT_QUANTITY>` of `<EQUIPMENT_NAME>` (for in-kind sponsorship)**]** for `<EVENT_NAME>` scheduled on `<EVENT_DATE>` at `<LOCATION>`. 

First of all, this event provides an immense opportunity for us to showcase our talents/inventions. There are an estimated `<GUEST_COUNT>` guests in attendance. Below are the advantages of attending this event: 

- Free publicity to the guests present at the event
- Cash pool of `<PRIZE_AMOUNT>` for the winners
- **[OPTIONAL:** A chance to compete internationally if our team is selected**]**
- School name and logo on all the event websites alongside the club name and logo
- Certificate of participation, a plaque, and/or a trophy

**[OPTIONAL - Include only if a mentor is involved:** Furthermore, we are aided by our mentor `<MENTOR_NAME>`, who has led the club in similar events before. Attached is `<HIS/HER/THEIR>` CV, as well as the recommendation letter signed by the previous `<BOARD_MEMBER_TITLE>`.**]** All item costs, transportation costs, and miscellaneous spending will be fully itemised and sent to your desk for approval to ensure full transparency. 

We will send the itemised list of equipment needed shortly. The `<CLUB_NAME>` sincerely hopes you give us your approval to tackle this project head-on. We will follow up in person or via email on `<FOLLOW_UP_DATE>`.

Sincerely, 

`<SIGNATURE>`

`<SENDER_NAME>`  
`<CLUB_POSITION>`  
`<EMAIL_ADDRESS>`  
`<PHONE_NUMBER>`
